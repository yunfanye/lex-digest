# One-liner

Ryan Schiller explains Librex, an anonymous campus discussion app, and the difficult tradeoff between giving students room to say unpopular things and preventing anonymity from turning a community into harassment, rumor, and factional warfare.

# Summary

Students may express political or personal views they would avoid under their real names, which can reveal opinions hidden by campus status pressures. Without identity, users can attack people or spread claims cheaply, forcing product design and moderation to decide which harms justify limiting speech.

Unlike a giant public network, a university app connects people who share classes, dorms, friends, and institutions, so online conflict can quickly spill into offline life. Schiller's project assumes that software can create room for disagreement, but the quality of that room still depends on whether users value curiosity over humiliation.
