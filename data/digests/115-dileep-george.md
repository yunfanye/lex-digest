# One-liner

Dileep George argues for AI systems inspired by the computational principles of cortex, focusing on hierarchical representations, prediction, explaining away, and the gap between today's pattern recognition and flexible human reasoning.

# Summary

George is interested in the fact that large areas of cortex share similar circuitry, which hints that a common learning mechanism may support vision, language, memory, and reasoning. The discussion emphasizes explaining away: when one hypothesis accounts for an observation, the system should reduce support for competing explanations, a behavior important for structured perception.

George's work on hierarchical temporal memory and related models tries to encode objects and events as compositions whose parts recur across different contexts. The goal is to identify algorithms that make biological intelligence data-efficient and compositional, then express those ideas in engineered systems.
