# One-liner

Jason Calacanis discusses startups, angel investing, capitalism, media, friendship, and Silicon Valley, arguing that early-stage investing rewards people who can imagine an upside others dismiss while still surviving a portfolio full of failures.

# Summary

A small number of companies can return far more than the rest of a portfolio loses, so evaluating only the probability of failure can cause an investor to miss the companies that matter. Calacanis trains investors to explore the size of a possible success before listing every way an unconventional company could fail.

He defends entrepreneurial competition while acknowledging that a system loses legitimacy if large groups experience only insecurity while gains accumulate elsewhere. The episode includes advice and relationships with other technology figures, showing how founders often need people who can challenge their thinking without turning every conversation into a transaction.
